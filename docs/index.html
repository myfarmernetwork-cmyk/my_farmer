Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how My Farmer (“we”, “us”) processes personal data when you use our mobile application My Farmer (Android package com.myfarmer.network).

My Farmer is an agricultural marketplace. Product payments between buyers and sellers are peer-to-peer (cash, direct PayPal, bank transfer) — we do not process marketplace payments. Optional Premium features are billed via Google Play and managed with RevenueCat.

1. Data controller

Operator of the My Farmer app is the data controller under the EU GDPR.

  • App: My Farmer (com.myfarmer.network)
  • Contact: myfarmer.network@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

  • Account: name, email, phone, address, role, farm/business details
  • Usage: products, orders, chat, animal records, land parcels, scan history
  • Payment profiles: PayPal email, masked card digits, IBAN (voluntary, for seller profiles)
  • Device: FCM push token, device ID for trial limits
  • Media: photos you upload; camera input for the plant scanner

3. Location data & OpenWeather API

With your permission, we access GPS location to show nearby marketplace listings, apply correct currency/pricing for your region, and save farm coordinates you choose to share.

For farmer weather alerts and widgets, we may send your coordinates (latitude/longitude) to OpenWeather and/or Open-Meteo to retrieve forecast data. We do not sell location data. You may deny location permission; some features will be limited.

Legal basis: consent (Android location permission) and/or contract performance for features you request (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)/(b)).

4. Plant scanner — camera usage

The AI plant scanner requires camera access (or gallery photos you select). Images are sent to our backend (Firebase Cloud Functions) and to Plant.id for identification. Images are used only to provide scan results and are not used for advertising.

Legal basis: consent when you open the scanner or pick a photo (Art. 6(1)(a)).

5. Firebase (Google)

We use Google Firebase (project my-farmer-552a3):

  • Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging (push)
  • Cloud Functions (account deletion, plant ID proxy, weather alerts, satellite data)

See Firebase Privacy and Google Privacy Policy.

6. Premium subscriptions — RevenueCat & Google Play

Premium subscriptions and licences are purchased through Google Play Billing. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to validate entitlements and sync subscription status to Firestore.

  • Processed: purchase tokens, subscription active/expiry, billing country, app user ID
  • Stored by us: premiumActive, subscriptionTier, subscriptionExpiresAt

Marketplace product payments are not handled by RevenueCat. Cancel renewals in Google Play → Subscriptions. Refunds follow Google Play terms.

RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy

7. Account deletion

You may delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete account, or email myfarmer.network@gmail.com.

Without active subscription

Account and personal data are deleted promptly (Firebase Auth, profile, payment methods, hidden listings).

With active Premium subscription

Deletion is scheduled for the end of the current billing period (Google Play / RevenueCat expiry). Until then you may use Premium you paid for; your public profile is hidden. After expiry, data is permanently removed.

Note: Deleting your account does not auto-cancel Google Play billing — cancel separately in Play Store.

8. Other third parties

  • Google Maps — maps and geocoding
  • Sentinel Hub / Copernicus — satellite NIRS/NDVI (via our servers)

9. Your rights (GDPR)

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and complaint to a supervisory authority.

Contact: myfarmer.network@gmail.com

10. Security & retention

HTTPS encryption, Firebase security rules. Data kept while account is active; removed or anonymized after deletion unless law requires longer retention.

11. Children

Not directed at children under 16. Contact us if a child submitted data.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected here and in the app where appropriate.

My Farmer ist ein landwirtschaftlicher Marktplatz. Zahlungen für Produkte zwischen Käufern und Verkäufern erfolgen peer-to-peer (Barzahlung, direktes PayPal, Überweisung) — wir verarbeiten keine Marktplatz-Zahlungen. Optionale Premium-Funktionen werden über Google Play abgerechnet und mit RevenueCat verwaltet.

Betreiber der My-Farmer-App ist Verantwortlicher im Sinne der EU-DSGVO.

Mit Ihrer Einwilligung greifen wir auf GPS-Standort zu, um Marktplatz-Angebote in der Nähe anzuzeigen, passende Währung/Preise für Ihre Region anzuwenden und von Ihnen freigegebene Hofkoordinaten zu speichern.

Rechtsgrundlage: Einwilligung (Android-Standortberechtigung) und/oder Vertragserfüllung für von Ihnen genutzte Funktionen (DSGVO Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a/b).

4. Pflanzenscanner — Kameranutzung

Der KI-Pflanzenscanner benötigt Kamerazugriff (oder Fotos aus der Galerie). Bilder werden an unser Backend (Firebase Cloud Functions) und an Plant.id zur Bestimmung gesendet. Bilder dienen nur dem Scan-Ergebnis und werden nicht für Werbung genutzt.

Rechtsgrundlage: Einwilligung beim Öffnen des Scanners oder Auswählen eines Fotos (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a).

Marktplatz-Produktzahlungen laufen nicht über RevenueCat. Kündigen Sie Verlängerungen in Google Play → Abos. Erstattungen gemäß Google-Play-Bedingungen.

RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy

7. Kontolöschung

Sie können Ihr Konto in der App löschen: Profil → Konto löschen, oder per E-Mail an myfarmer.network@gmail.com.

Konto und personenbezogene Daten werden umgehend gelöscht (Firebase Auth, Profil, Zahlungsmethoden, ausgeblendete Anzeigen).

Ova Politika privatnosti objašnjava kako My Farmer („mi“, „nas“) obrađuje osobne podatke kada koristite mobilnu aplikaciju My Farmer (Android paket com.myfarmer.network).

My Farmer je poljoprivredna tržnica. Plaćanja za proizvode između kupaca i prodavača su peer-to-peer (gotovina, direktan PayPal, bankovni transfer) — mi ne procesiramo plaćanja na tržnici. Premium funkcije se naplaćuju putem Google Playa i upravljaju se putem RevenueCata.

Operater aplikacije My Farmer je voditelj obrade u smislu EU GDPR-a.

  • Načini plaćanja: PayPal email, maskirane znamenke kartice, IBAN (dobrovoljno, za profil prodavača)
  • Uređaj: FCM token za push, ID uređaja za limite probnih skenova

Za vremenske alarme i widget farmera možemo poslati vaše koordinate (geografska širina/dužina) servisima OpenWeather i/ili Open-Meteo radi dohvaćanja prognoze. Lokacijske podatke ne prodajemo. Možete odbiti dozvolu za lokaciju; neke funkcije tada neće raditi.

Pravna osnova: privola (Android dozvola za lokaciju) i/ili izvršenje ugovora za tražene funkcije (GDPR čl. 6(1)(a)/(b)).

4. AI skener biljaka — korištenje kamere

AI skener biljaka zahtijeva pristup kameri (ili fotografijama koje odaberete iz galerije). Slike se šalju na naš backend (Firebase Cloud Functions) i servisu Plant.id radi identifikacije. Slike služe isključivo za rezultat skeniranja i ne koriste se za oglašavanje.
